Support

Help with the FarziPedia iPhone app and Chrome extension.

❧

Contact us

  • Email: faraaz@elixarsystems.com

Using the iPhone app

  1. Copy the link to a video that has captions, then paste it into the field at the top of the app.
  2. Tap Render the Codex and keep the app open while it works. It reads along with the video, captures the key moments as pictures, and writes the post. This usually takes one to three minutes.
  3. The finished post opens by itself and is saved in your library on the home screen.
  4. To share a post, tap the share button at the top right and choose PDF or Markdown.
  5. To delete a post from your phone, swipe left on it in the library.

Every post is also published to the free public library on farzipedia.com, so only render videos you're happy to see listed publicly. No account or API key is needed.

Common problems

  • "Too few snippets to write from." The video has no captions, or very short ones. Try a different video.
  • "Only some frames captured cleanly." Keep the app open and on screen for the whole render; switching apps or locking the phone pauses the video.
  • "Couldn't publish to farzipedia.com." The post is still saved on your phone. Check your connection and render again later if you'd like it in the public library.
  • Livestreams and private videos aren't supported, and videos shorter than 30 seconds are too short to write about.

Removing a post from farzipedia.com

Email faraaz@elixarsystems.com with the post's link (the /blog/<id> address). We delete it within seven days.

Privacy

FarziPedia has no accounts, ads, or analytics. Read exactly what is sent where in our privacy policy.
